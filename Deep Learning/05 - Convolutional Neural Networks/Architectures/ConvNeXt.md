**ConvNeXt** is a modern CNN architecture designed to be **competitive with Transformer-based models**  on image classification benchmarks, while remaining a pure convolutional network.

## Key Features

- Uses **connection structures between layers** that enable more effective learning of spatial and semantic relationships (inspired by the design principles of Swin Transformer)
- Demonstrates that a well-designed CNN can match or exceed the performance of transformers on vision tasks

## Significance

ConvNeXt proves that the performance gains of Vision Transformers were not solely due to the attention mechanism, but largely attributable to modern training procedures and architectural improvements that can also be applied to CNNs.
