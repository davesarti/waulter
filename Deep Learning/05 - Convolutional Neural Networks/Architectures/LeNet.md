**LeNet** was the milestone that initiated the field of Convolutional Neural Networks. It was instrumental in promoting CNNs for character recognition and other computer vision tasks.

## Key Features

- Alternating **convolutional** and **subsampling (pooling)** layers, followed by fully connected layers
- Requires **complete retraining when image resolution changes**
- Input: 32×32
