**GNMT** (Google Neural Machine Translation) is a production-scale seq2seq system that closed most of the gap between Neural Machine Translation and human-level translation on several language pairs.

![[Pasted image 20260707095137.png]]

## Architecture

### Encoder

- **Layer 1**: Bidirectional LSTM — captures both left and right context; its forward and backward outputs are summed
- **Layers 2–8**: 7 stacked unidirectional LSTMs (left-to-right)
- **Residual connections**: applied from layer 3 onwards to stabilise gradient flow in the deep stack

### Decoder

- 8 stacked unidirectional LSTMs
- **Attention**: computed between the **bottom decoder layer** (layer 1) and the **top encoder layer** (layer 8), feeding the context vector into all decoder layers

### Residual Connections

For layers $i \geq 3$, each LSTM layer receives the sum of its input and its output from the previous layer:
$$x^{i+1}_t = \text{LSTM}^i(x^i_t) + x^i_t$$
This allows gradients to bypass layers, making 8-layer training feasible.

## Training

Train is optimized through a loss function that improve the BLEU score
