## Activation Functions

An **activation function** $\sigma$ introduces non-linearity into the network, allowing it to learn complex, non-linear mappings from input to output. Without activation functions, a deep network would collapse into a single linear transformation.

For the **output layer**, [[Softmax]] serves a similar role — it is technically an activation applied to the final linear transformation, but operates on the full output vector rather than element-wise, producing a probability distribution.
