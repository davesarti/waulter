## Core Idea

Instead of relying on a single model, **train several models separately** and have all of them **vote on the output** for test examples.

Different models will usually make different errors on the test set. Combining their predictions averages out individual errors, producing a better overall result.

---

## Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating)

The most common implementation of Model Ensembles:

1. Train **several instances of the same model type**
2. Each instance is trained on a **random subset of the training set (with replacement)**
3. **Combine all predictions** (e.g., majority vote for classification, average for regression)

---

## Trade-offs

| Pro | Con |
|---|---|
| Better overall accuracy | More computational resources required |
| Reduces variance | More complex to implement and manage |
| Averages out individual model errors | Inference is slower (multiple forward passes) |

---

## Relation to Dropout

[[04.9 - Dropout|Dropout]] can be seen as an efficient approximation of ensemble methods — it implicitly trains an exponentially large number of neural networks that share parameters, without the computational cost of explicit bagging.
