## Core Idea

Data augmentation improves model generalization by **increasing the amount and variety of training data** through transformations of existing samples. More training data → better generalization to unseen data.

The key insight: **the classifier's main task is to be invariant to a wide range of transformations of the input data**.

---

## Application to Images

The most common domain. Typical transformations:
- **Rotation**
- **Translation**
- **Color change**
- **Scaling**
- **Flipping**

Goal: create a diversity of samples covering as much of the input data space as possible, helping the model learn a more robust and general representation of discriminative features.

---

## Application to Other Data Types

**Acoustic data:**
- Pitch shift
- Time stretching
- Dynamic range compression

**Textual data:**
- Word substitution / deletion
- Letter insertion
- Synonym replacement
- Adjective modification

---

## Why It Works

By exposing the model to many variations of the same sample, the **model is prevented from memorizing exact training examples**. It is forced to learn the underlying structure, reducing overfitting.
