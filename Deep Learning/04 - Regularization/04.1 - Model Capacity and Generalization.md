## Model Capacity

The **capability of a model** refers to its **ability to fit a wide range of functions**.
- **Low capacity** → difficulty adapting to the training set → **underfitting**
- **High capacity** → fits training data too well, including noise → **overfitting** → poor generalization

Model capacity is closely related to the **hypothesis space**: the set of all features the model can potentially learn. A higher-capacity model has access to a larger hypothesis space, allowing it to learn more complex relationships.

## The Generalization Gap

As model complexity increases:
- Training loss **decreases** monotonically
- Generalization loss (test loss) **decreases then increases** **again**

The gap between training and generalization loss is the **generalization gap**.

## Dealing with Overfitting

The goal is to find a **balance between model complexity and generalization capacity**:
- Capacity high enough to capture true regularities in training data
- But not so high that it captures spurious regularities or noise
