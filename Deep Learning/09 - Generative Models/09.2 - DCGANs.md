**DCGAN** (Deep Convolutional GAN) is the first [[09.1 - Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)|GAN]] architecture based on deep [[05.2 - Inside a CNN|convolutional networks]], and the first model to generate high-resolution images using a GAN.

## Architecture

### Discriminator

The Discriminator differs from a classical CNN in that it uses **only strided convolutions, no pooling**. Pooling makes discrimination *too easy*, hurting the Discriminator's ability to judge whether an image is real or fake.

Additional design choices:
- [[Leaky ReLU]] as the activation function.
- Only **one fully connected layer** before the output.
- [[Batch normalization]] after most layers, for faster training.

## Arithmetic of GANs

Exploring the latent space of a DCGAN trained on celebrity faces revealed that:

- The Generator maps points in the latent space to specific images — a mapping that differs every time the model is retrained.
- The Generator supports **vector arithmetic** on faces: e.g., *(smiling woman) − (neutral woman) + (neutral man) = smiling man*.

This works because arithmetic is performed directly on the latent-space points corresponding to each face, demonstrating that the Generator has learned meaningful **interpolation** capabilities — combining latent vectors yields samples whose features are a combination of the inputs.
