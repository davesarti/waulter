## Motivation

As the community began applying [[06.3 - Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)|LSTM]] models to tasks such as captioning, it became apparent that the network was overly complex. GRU was introduced to address this complexity by **simplifying the LSTM cell by removing one of its gates**.

Despite having only two gates, the model is sufficiently resilient to the vanishing gradient problem. Notably, the GRU stores all information flow in the **hidden state $h_t$ only** — there is no separate memory cell $c_t$.

## Gates

![[Pasted image 20260705185650.png]]

> [!warning] Reset Gate
> Used to decide how much of the passed information to forget.
> Its output $r_t$ is computed by the sigmoid function applied to $x_t$ and $h_{t-1}$, with its own parameters $W_r$:
> $$r_t = \sigma\!\left(W_r \begin{pmatrix} x_t \\ h_{t-1} \end{pmatrix} + b_r\right)$$

> [!example] Update Gate
> Helps the model determine how much of the past information (from previous time steps) needs to be transmitted to the future.
> Its output $z_t$ is computed by the sigmoid function applied to $x_t$ and $h_{t-1}$, with its own parameters $W_z$:
> $$z_t = \sigma\!\left(W_z \begin{pmatrix} x_t \\ h_{t-1} \end{pmatrix} + b_z\right)$$

---

## Input Modulation

A key change from the LSTM is that the value of the input modulation $h'_t$ is **no longer based solely on the complete $h_{t-1}$**, but instead on a "restricted stream" determined by the reset gate $r_t$:

$$h'_t = \tanh W\!\begin{pmatrix} x_t \\ r_t \odot h_{t-1} \end{pmatrix}$$

The reset gate acts as a filter on the previous hidden state before computing new candidate information.

---

## Final Hidden State

The final hidden state is a blend of the old state and the new candidate, controlled by the update gate:

$$h_t = (1 - z_t) \odot h_{t-1} + z_t \odot h'_t$$

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $(1 - z_t) \odot h_{t-1}$ | How much of the **past** state to carry forward |
| $z_t \odot h'_t$ | How much of the **new candidate** to incorporate |

The update gate therefore plays the combined role of both the forget and input gates in the LSTM, using a single complementary weighting.
