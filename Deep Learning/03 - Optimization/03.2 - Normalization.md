## Overview

Deep neural networks are difficult to train partly due to **Covariate Shift** — the distribution of a layer's inputs changes during training as the parameters of the preceding layers evolve. When most inputs fall into non-linear regions of the activation function, the learning process slows down significantly.

Normalization techniques address this by standardizing activations at each layer. All methods share the same core structure: subtract the mean, divide by the standard deviation, then apply learnable **scale** ($\gamma$) and **shift** ($\beta$) parameters to restore expressive power.

The key difference between them is *which dimensions* are used to compute the statistics.
